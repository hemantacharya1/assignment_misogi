Document: How to Submit a Feature Request
We love hearing what features would make your experience better. If you have a request, head to our Feature Requests board in the Help Center. Click Submit a Feature and describe your idea clearly—what it does, why it matters to you, and how it would improve your workflow.

The more context you provide, the easier it is for our product team to understand the request and evaluate its impact. You can also browse and upvote other ideas submitted by the community.

Once submitted, you’ll get email updates when the status changes (e.g., under review, planned, released). While we can’t promise every idea will be built, we take customer input seriously and regularly review top-voted requests.

For enterprise customers, you can also discuss roadmap influence during your quarterly check-ins with your account manager.
