Document: Understanding Your Monthly Invoice
Your monthly invoice summarizes all billable activity for the previous 30 days. This includes your subscription plan, usage-based charges (if any), and applicable taxes. You can view and download your invoices by visiting the Billing section in your dashboard.

The Invoice Summary shows your base plan and any overages (e.g., extra API calls or storage). Below that, you'll find a breakdown by service, with line items like “Pro Plan Subscription,” “Excess API Usage,” and “Additional Seats.”

If something doesn’t look right, double-check your usage logs, which are available under the Usage tab. These logs are updated in near-real time and help you track usage patterns across your team.

Still have questions? Reach out to our billing team via the Contact Support button in the Billing section. We usually reply within one business day.

Document: Requesting a Refund for a Billing Error
If you believe there’s a mistake on your bill, you can request a refund within 14 days of the charge. To get started, go to your Billing page and click on the invoice in question. Use the Report an Issue button to send a message directly to our billing team.

In your message, please include the invoice number, a short explanation of the issue, and any relevant context (e.g., unexpected plan change or duplicate charge). This helps us investigate and respond more quickly.

Refunds are typically processed within 3–5 business days once the request is approved. You’ll get a confirmation email once it’s done.

If the charge came through a third-party reseller or payment platform (e.g., Apple Pay or Stripe Connect), refund times may vary slightly based on their processing timelines.
